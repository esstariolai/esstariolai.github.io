How to play

  1. Holes on each atom show how many bonds it makes: H 1, O 2, N 3, C 4.
  2. On your turn press Start and build the molecule from the kit. One stick = single bond, two sticks = double, three = triple.
  3. Press Done. Everyone checks your build against the answer. Wrong build? Press Wrong (+30 s).
  4. Take the molecule apart and pass the kit on. After a few rounds the lowest average time wins.

One kit (6 H, 2 C, 2 O, 2 N, 10 sticks) builds every molecule here. Two kits = head-to-head duel on the same card.
